CCTV SECURITY

See More. Protect More. Respond Faster.

A modern CCTV system does more than record video. It provides visibility across your facilities, helps monitor critical areas and gives your organization access to recorded and live footage when it matters.

Badee Solutions designs and deploys complete CCTV surveillance systems for offices, commercial facilities, warehouses, buildings and other business environments, using the appropriate combination of cameras, recording systems, storage, networking and monitoring technologies.

VIDEO SURVEILLANCE

A Complete Surveillance Environment

A professional CCTV system consists of more than cameras. The cameras, recording equipment, storage, network or cabling infrastructure, power, displays and monitoring software must work together as one system.

A complete surveillance solution can include:

  • IP or Analog Cameras
  • NVR or DVR
  • Surveillance HDD
  • PoE Switches
  • Power Supplies
  • Network or Coaxial Cabling
  • Monitors and Displays
  • Camera Mounts and Brackets
  • Video Management Software
  • Mobile Monitoring
  • Desktop Monitoring
  • Network Infrastructure

Designed around your site

Badee designs the complete environment around your site, coverage requirements, recording needs and operational objectives.

CCTV TECHNOLOGY

Hikvision & Dahua Surveillance Solutions

Badee can design and deploy CCTV environments using established surveillance technologies from Hikvision and Dahua, selecting the appropriate equipment based on the site, coverage requirements, recording duration and operational needs.

Hikvision CCTV

Hikvision provides a broad portfolio of video surveillance technologies covering cameras, recording systems, storage, video management and related surveillance infrastructure. Solutions can include IP cameras, analog cameras, NVRs, DVRs, surveillance storage, video management, mobile monitoring and network/PoE infrastructure.

Dahua CCTV

Dahua provides video surveillance technologies covering cameras, recorders, storage, video management and supporting surveillance infrastructure. Solutions can include IP cameras, analog cameras, NVRs, DVRs, surveillance storage, video management and mobile monitoring.

Network & PoE Infrastructure

The appropriate platform and equipment are selected based on the customer's site, coverage, recording, integration and operational requirements.

SURVEILLANCE CAMERAS

The Right Camera for Every Area

Different areas require different camera types, resolutions, viewing angles and protection levels. We design camera placement around the areas that need to be monitored and the level of detail required.

IP Cameras

Network-based cameras that transmit video over IP networks and can integrate with modern NVR, VMS and network infrastructure.

Analog Cameras

Traditional CCTV cameras using coaxial infrastructure and compatible DVR recording systems.

Indoor Cameras

Cameras designed for offices, corridors, reception areas, retail environments and other indoor locations.

Outdoor Cameras

Cameras designed for external environments with appropriate weather and environmental protection.

Fixed Cameras

Fixed-view cameras for monitoring defined areas and specific points of interest.

PTZ Cameras

Pan-Tilt-Zoom cameras that provide remote control of camera direction and zoom on supported models.

Specialized Cameras

Specialized surveillance cameras can be selected for environments requiring specific features such as enhanced low-light performance, wider coverage or other supported capabilities.
