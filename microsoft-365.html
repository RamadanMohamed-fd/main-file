WHAT'S INCLUDED

Microsoft 365 Across Your Business

Microsoft 365 is more than email and Office applications. It brings together productivity, collaboration, identity, security, device management and cloud services within a connected Microsoft ecosystem.

Microsoft 365 Business

Business plans designed for organizations that need secure productivity and collaboration tools. Examples include Business Basic, Business Standard, Business Premium and Apps for business.

Microsoft 365 & Office 365 Enterprise

Enterprise plans designed for organizations with broader productivity, security, compliance and management requirements. Examples include Microsoft 365 E3 and E5, Office 365 E1, E3 and E5, and Microsoft 365 F3 for frontline scenarios.

MICROSOFT 365 TECHNOLOGIES

More Than Email and Office

A Microsoft 365 environment can span productivity, collaboration, identity, devices, security, data and AI rather than simply email and Office applications.

Exchange Online

Business email and calendaring delivered through Microsoft's cloud platform.

Microsoft Teams

Communication and collaboration through chat, meetings, calling and teamwork.

SharePoint

Secure collaboration, document management and business content management.

OneDrive

Cloud file storage and sharing designed for individual work and collaboration.

Microsoft Entra ID

Identity and access management that helps control who can access business resources.

Microsoft Intune

Cloud-based endpoint and device management to help manage users, devices and applications.

Microsoft Defender

Security capabilities that help protect identities, endpoints, email and Microsoft 365 environments.

Microsoft Purview

Data security, governance, compliance and information protection capabilities across the Microsoft ecosystem.

Copilot

AI-powered capabilities designed to help users work more efficiently across Microsoft applications and services.
