CHOOSING THE RIGHT PLATFORM

Fortinet or Sophos?

Both platforms provide next-generation firewall capabilities. The right choice depends on your organization's network architecture, security requirements, existing technologies, users, locations and operational model.

Badee can assess your environment and recommend the appropriate platform based on your actual requirements rather than selecting a firewall based only on brand or appliance size.

Fortinet may be considered when:

  • Multiple sites and network architectures need to be connected
  • Network performance and scalability are key considerations
  • The organization has complex network environments
  • Secure networking and SD-WAN capabilities are important
  • A broader Fortinet security ecosystem is relevant

Sophos may be considered when:

  • Ease of administration is an important consideration
  • The organization already uses Sophos security technologies
  • Integrated endpoint and network security is valuable
  • The environment requires centralized security management
  • The organization wants to simplify security operations
