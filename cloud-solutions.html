CLOUD COMPUTING

The Cloud, Built Around Your Business

Cloud computing provides on-demand access to computing resources such as servers, storage, databases, networking, applications and other technology services.

Instead of building every capability around physical infrastructure, organizations can use cloud environments to deploy workloads, scale resources and access technology according to their requirements.

Broad Workload Support

Cloud platforms support a wide range of use cases, including applications, databases, storage, analytics, backup, disaster recovery and development environments.

Resources On Demand

Deploy workloads and scale compute, storage and networking resources according to your actual business requirements.
