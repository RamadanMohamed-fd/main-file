ROOM TYPES

The Right Solution for Every Room

Huddle Rooms

Small spaces designed for quick meetings, discussions and remote collaboration. Typically a compact display, conference camera, microphone, speaker and room controller.

Small Meeting Rooms

Designed for small teams and regular video meetings. Typically a display, camera, microphone, speakers, collaboration system and wireless presentation.

Medium Meeting Rooms

Designed for larger teams, presentations and more structured video meetings.

Large Conference Rooms

Designed for larger groups, executive meetings, presentations and high-quality hybrid collaboration. Typically a large or dual display, PTZ or intelligent camera, multiple microphones, speakers, touch controller, presentation system and room scheduling.

Boardrooms

Premium meeting environments designed for executive meetings, decision-making and high-quality hybrid collaboration. Typically multiple displays, an advanced camera system, ceiling or distributed microphones, professional audio and room control.

Training & Collaboration Rooms

Flexible rooms designed for presentations, workshops, training sessions and interactive collaboration, with a presentation system and multiple connectivity options.
